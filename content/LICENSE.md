Changes: the lists were combined, seven vulgar words and "quadrillion" were removed (`sources/skip.txt`), and
original part-of-speech labels, English definitions, example sentences and Turkish translations were added
(`authored/words_*.tsv`), together with original phrasal verb entries, grammar lessons and pronunciation notes.
`sources/NGSL_12_definitions.csv` is the NGSL's own definition file, kept for reference only; the app does not use it.
