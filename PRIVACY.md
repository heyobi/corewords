# Privacy

Corewords collects no data.

- No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
- Your progress (which words you know, review dates, settings) is stored only on your device, in the app's own
  storage. Deleting the app deletes it.
- Pronunciation audio is spoken by the voices built into iOS. Nothing is downloaded or uploaded.
- The optional "Say it" exercise uses Apple's speech recognition **on the device only**
  (`requiresOnDeviceRecognition`). The microphone is used only while you hold the button. Nothing is recorded,
  stored or sent anywhere. If your device can't recognize English offline, the exercise is simply not offered.
- Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.
- The one-time Pro purchase is handled by the App Store. The app only learns whether Pro is unlocked.
